feat(page): the station name in the heading is the field you type into

The heading said "from GRANTHAM" next to a button labelled "Change", so a
reader went looking for the word "Change" on the page, and the first-use note
had to spend its first clause on naming the control because the control would
not name itself. Choosing a station was three gestures and lost the budget on
the way, and it could not be typed into at all.

Clicking the name now moves the station field into the heading, where the name
was, and the list filters as it is typed into. The field is as wide as its
placeholder and grows with what is written in it, so an empty heading is not a
hole where a control should be. The options are still real anchors to that
station's page carrying the budget, ticket type and facets, so changing where a
reader is leaving from is a correction of the decision they have already made
rather than a reset of it.

Escape closes it, and it closes it from the field. The keydown listener was on
the panel, and the field is moved out of the panel and into the heading while
the picker is open, so Escape -- pressed in the only place a reader can press
it -- bubbled past the listener and did nothing. The browser suite had a check
that pressed the station control a second time to close the picker; it timed
out, because the control is the field now and is not on the page to press.

  python -m unittest tests.test_build_demo_page -v
