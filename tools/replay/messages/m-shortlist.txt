fix(page): one shortlist control, and the list where the action is

Every row carried a button reading "Shortlist". Twenty rows is twenty copies of
the same word, repeated on the rows the reader was not choosing; pressing one
changed the button's own text, so the row changed width and the ground shifted
under a reader who was comparing rows; and the list it produced was a section
below the table, 205px under the fold, so the confirmation was somewhere other
than where the reader was looking.

One select above the table replaces all of them, and it lists exactly the
destinations on the page being looked at. A place already on the strip is not
offered again: the strip is the record of what is held, and a second way to add
the same place is a control that silently does nothing. Each chip is the
station's name, its price, and a way to take it off the list; the name is a
button that takes the reader back to the row, because a chip is a way back
rather than a destination.

The strip sits above the count line, not between it and the table. The count
describes the rows under it, and the two were 89px apart with a row of controls
in the gap.

Eight places is the limit and the limit is said on the page, not only to a
screen reader: a control that stops responding with no explanation is the one
thing a reader cannot act on.
