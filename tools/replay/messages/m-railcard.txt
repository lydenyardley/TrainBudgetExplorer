fix(page): the railcard's answer belongs in the row it is about

Choosing a card opened a dialog. It was a second surface on a page that already
has one per row, it took the reader away from the fare they were looking at, and
it threw a ReferenceError on every single page load, because the listener that
wired it up was never removed with the element it listened to.

The note is a paragraph in the row's own panel now, beside the times and the
condition it qualifies, and it is only there when a card is chosen: with no card
selected the control above already says the prices are standard, and a sentence
on every expanded row repeating that is the noise this replaced. Where the
reader picks a card the note names it, because the page lists six.

The sentence is a sentence. "Restricted fare — condition below" and "Unrestricted
Anytime Day Return; price and availability can change" were read as two facts of
equal weight, and the second half was the one a reader needed before deciding
whether to trust the price at all. A row whose fare carries no condition is not
given a condition paragraph to read.

  python -m unittest tests.test_build_demo_page -v
