refactor(page): say less, and put the legal text where legal text goes

The page carried six provenance paragraphs above its table and two more under
it, and the first-use note was a second paragraph saying roughly what the first
one said. A reader who came for a price met a build fingerprint first.

What is left above the table is the mode note and the freshness line: the two
facts that are about the numbers on screen. Everything that describes the build
-- the price source, the reference feed, the build fingerprint -- is in one
disclosure in the footer that says what it holds. That is the same trade
ADR-0040 made for the ticket link, extended to the rest: the claim has to be
readable, not rendered in full above the thing it qualifies.

The heading loses its middle dots, and the copy loses the sentences that only
existed to explain the copy.

The first-use note is now one sentence: "Click the station name to start from
somewhere else." A one-origin build has no such control, so it says what is
true there instead of pointing at a word the page does not carry.

  python -m unittest tests.test_build_demo_page -v
