fix(fixture): price the six reviewed cards, with their own bounds

The synthetic feed carried one railcard, under the code TWO, which is not in the
reviewed set at all. A page built from it offered a select with one option in
it -- the control that exists to offer six -- and the bug behind it was hidden
by a second accident: the railcard rules were written for YNG, so a card with no
rule of its own is not discounted at all, and the page could not tell the
difference between "this feed prices no card" and "this feed prices one card".

The fixture now carries the six reviewed cards with the party each one prices,
and one RR record per card, so the party filter has something to take out: Two
Together cannot price one traveller, and the Student Discount Card cannot price
two. A page built from it exercises the bounds, the menu and the help, which is
what a fixture is for.

Read by running:

  python -m unittest tests.test_atoc_fares -v
  python build_demo_page.py --feed _demo_feed --timetable _demo_feed --all-origins --out _demo_site/index.html
