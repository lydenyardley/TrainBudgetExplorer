**Subject:** Licensing enquiry — public fares page under an RSP Data Licence, and a
Darwin timetable permitted-purpose question

Dear Rail Delivery Group,

I am building a small, free-to-use website that answers one question: from a chosen
station, which destinations are reachable by return train for under a given budget. It is
a personal project, currently not published. It would show a station name, a price, and a
link to a ticket retailer; I also intend to join an affiliate programme, so the page would
earn commission on some ticket sales. I would rather establish the licence position than
publish and hope.

I already hold four Rail Data Marketplace licences and have read their terms: Darwin
Timetable Files, Knowledgebase Ticket Types, Knowledgebase Ticket Restrictions, and Fare
Locations Reference Data v1.3. Two questions remain, and I would be grateful for your
guidance on both.

**1. The Darwin Timetable Files licence, Schedule 1 §5.** Under `PERMITTED PURPOSES` it
states: "The raw data may be copied and used by Data Consumer for internal business
purposes only." The Open Government Licence head of the same document, by contrast,
permits exploiting the Information commercially and non-commercially, including within
my own product.

I have noticed that the three Rail Data Marketplace products I hold for ticket types,
ticket restrictions and fare locations each carry a *different* §5 — "The raw data may be
made freely available or otherwise distributed to third parties" — under the same
Schedule 1 template. It therefore looks as though the permitted purpose is set
deliberately per product.

My question is whether the Darwin restriction is intended to prevent a publicly
accessible page from displaying journey times, or whether it is aimed at restricting
redistribution of the raw feed itself. If a public page is not permitted under the current
terms, I would be very grateful to know whether any variant or additional permission is
available. I fully accept the answer, and if it is no then the site will be published
without journey times, or not at all.

**2. Fares data under an RSP Data Licence.** The fares data I need is specified in
RSPS5045. I understand from BR Fares Ltd's licensing page that a party publishing fares
information to the public may need to hold its own RSP Data Licence, and that these are
described there as generally free of charge. However, the published template
(`RSP_Data_Licence_Template_v07-00`) provides in clause 8.1 for a Licence Fee and, if
applicable, a Datafeeds Charge, with the amount left blank in the Schedule to be completed
per licensee. I would therefore like to ask:

- a. Is there a free or Open-licensed fares product on the Rail Data Marketplace that
     would suit this use, which I may have missed?
- b. If not, is an RSP Data Licence the correct route, and what would it cost for one
     person publishing a public page of the kind described? Is there a lower band for
     small or non-commercial use?
- c. What lead time should I allow, and what do you need from me to apply?
- d. Is the schedule variant where I source the data from an approved third party, or the
     variant where RSP supplies the datafeeds, the one that would apply?

**3. Attribution, for whichever licence applies.** Schedule 1 §8 `ATTRIBUTION` lists no
organisations on any of my licences, while the Data Sharing Agreement terms make crediting
the Data Publisher a condition under clause 3.3.1. Could you confirm the wording and
placement you require — for example whether "Source: Rail Delivery Group" in the page
footer meets it — and whether the same or different wording is needed for the timetable
and the fares data? I would also like to confirm that a page showing only a fare code and
a price, and no ticket restriction text, falls outside the "Powered by National Rail
Enquiries" branding requirement.

Thank you for your time. I am happy to answer any questions, and I will not publish
anything until I have your answer.
